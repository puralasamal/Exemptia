Exemptia Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Exemptia helps students turn lessons into notes. It is built to collect as little as possible. Exemptia was founded and made by Purala Samal. Contact: [your email here].

What we do not collect

  • No account. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, address, contacts or photos.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. We never sell or share your data.
  • No audio recordings are saved anywhere.

Microphone

Exemptia uses the microphone only after you choose to start listening and allow it. A clear “Transcription active” label is always shown while it listens. Listening stops when you pause, when the class goes quiet (automatic pause), or when you end the class. To turn your speech into text, Exemptia uses your device’s built-in speech recognition, which is provided by your browser or phone (for example Google on Android). That provider’s own privacy policy applies to that step.

Your notes

Notes, doubts and summaries stay on your device, in the app’s memory, while it is open. They are not uploaded to our servers. The class transcript is deleted when the class ends. Closing the app or pressing “Delete everything” removes your notes. Notes only leave the app if you choose to copy or save them.

Off-topic speech

Sentences that are not study content (greetings, personal chat) are discarded immediately and never kept. Emails, phone numbers and meeting links are removed from text before it is processed.

AI features

If a version of Exemptia uses an AI service to improve notes or answer doubts, only the filtered study text is sent to that service to create the answer — never audio, names or contact details.

Children

Exemptia is designed for students and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Students should follow their school’s rules about transcribing classes.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
