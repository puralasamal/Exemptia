Exemptia

Your private AI study companion

Your privacy comes first. Exemptia only processes information needed for your study session.

  1. Start a classZoom, Google Meet, Webex, Teams or any other app. You choose what Exemptia can hear. Nothing starts on its own.
  2. Notes write themselvesLessons, definitions, formulas and examples are sorted for you. Chit-chat is thrown away.
  3. Ask, then reviseAsk a doubt any time. At the end you get a summary and practice questions.

Exemptia never asks for

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Address
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • An account

No audio is saved. Your notes stay in this browser tab until you close it or delete them.

  1. Open your phone’s Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions → Microphone and choose Allow.
